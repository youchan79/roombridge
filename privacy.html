Privacy

When you apply on this site, RoomBridge receives your name, organization, email address, phone number and country.

  • We use them only to reply to your application and to plan housing for your group.
  • We do not sell them or share them with anyone for advertising.
  • Your application reaches us by email through a form delivery service.
  • We delete them one year after our last contact, unless we sign a contract with your organization.
  • To see, correct or delete your details, reply to any email from us.
