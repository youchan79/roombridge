What one contract covers

One weekly price per room. You deal with us, not with each landlord.

  • A private studio for each person

    Every room has its own bathroom, kitchenette and washing machine.

  • Bedding and a starter kit

    Sheets, towels and basics ready on arrival day, plus storage for luggage.

  • Cleaning before and after

    We prepare each room before you arrive and send a professional crew after you leave.

  • Utilities included

    Electricity, gas and water are in the price, even in a cold winter.

  • A manager who speaks English

    One room is kept for our night manager, who answers within 10 minutes.

  • Insurance and damage handled

    We carry liability insurance. If something breaks, we repair it and settle it with the owner.
